This checkpoint focused on **Signed payload inspection**. It moved Webhook Replay Console toward its goal: Make integration recovery quick without turning replay into another source of duplicates.

## What changed

Turned the initial idea into a bounded problem with a clear result to work toward. I kept the change deliberately narrow so its effect could be checked before adding another layer.

## What I learned

The useful part of this checkpoint was making the work visible and repeatable instead of relying on a one-off result. The project is easier to reason about when each decision leaves behind a small piece of evidence: a test, a trace, a comparison, or a written constraint.
