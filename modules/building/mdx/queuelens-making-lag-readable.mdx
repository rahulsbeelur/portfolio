Raw lag numbers tell me that a consumer is behind, but not whether the situation is getting better or worse. Today I added a timeline and a short health summary so the first screen answers the practical question: “Do I need to act now?”

## What changed

Partitions are now grouped by consumer, and the dashboard separates a short traffic spike from lag that keeps growing. The next step is to test those rules against a real incident-shaped dataset.
