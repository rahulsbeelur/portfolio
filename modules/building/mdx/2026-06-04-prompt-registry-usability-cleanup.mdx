This checkpoint focused on **Ownership conventions**. It moved Prompt Registry toward its goal: Understand whether a central prompt registry improves collaboration enough to justify another platform.

## What changed

Removed avoidable choices and made the most common path faster to understand. I kept the change deliberately narrow so its effect could be checked before adding another layer.

-   Reduced setup steps
-   Improved labels and defaults
-   Removed one unnecessary configuration option

## What I learned

The useful part of this checkpoint was making the work visible and repeatable instead of relying on a one-off result. The project is easier to reason about when each decision leaves behind a small piece of evidence: a test, a trace, a comparison, or a written constraint.
