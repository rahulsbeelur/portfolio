This checkpoint focused on **Request-key storage**. It moved Idempotency Lab toward its goal: Provide a small, testable example of idempotent API design.

## What changed

Added explicit behaviour for invalid input, unavailable dependencies, and interrupted work. I kept the change deliberately narrow so its effect could be checked before adding another layer.

-   Added dependency failure states

## What I learned

The useful part of this checkpoint was making the work visible and repeatable instead of relying on a one-off result. The project is easier to reason about when each decision leaves behind a small piece of evidence: a test, a trace, a comparison, or a written constraint.
