This checkpoint focused on **Error-budget examples**. It moved SLO Starter Kit toward its goal: Help teams begin with useful reliability targets instead of abstract dashboards.

## What changed

Measured the slow path and tightened the part of the system most likely to fail under load. I kept the change deliberately narrow so its effect could be checked before adding another layer.

-   Measured the slowest operation
-   Reduced repeated work
-   Added a reliability regression check

## What I learned

The useful part of this checkpoint was making the work visible and repeatable instead of relying on a one-off result. The project is easier to reason about when each decision leaves behind a small piece of evidence: a test, a trace, a comparison, or a written constraint.
