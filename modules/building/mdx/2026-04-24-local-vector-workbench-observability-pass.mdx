This checkpoint focused on **Chunking experiments**. It moved Local Vector Workbench toward its goal: Make retrieval experiments private, quick, and reproducible on a laptop.

## What changed

Introduced the signals needed to understand what the system did and why a run failed. I kept the change deliberately narrow so its effect could be checked before adding another layer.

-   Added structured events
-   Recorded useful timings
-   Defined one actionable health signal

## What I learned

The useful part of this checkpoint was making the work visible and repeatable instead of relying on a one-off result. The project is easier to reason about when each decision leaves behind a small piece of evidence: a test, a trace, a comparison, or a written constraint.
