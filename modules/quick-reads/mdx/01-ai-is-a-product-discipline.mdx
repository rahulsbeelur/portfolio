---
title: AI is a product discipline, not a feature
summary: The useful question is not “where can we add AI?” It is “where does better judgment change the outcome?”
publishedAt: '2026-11-10'
area: AI
topics: ['Product strategy', 'AI systems']
accent: 'violet'
---

Start with the decision a person is trying to make. If the model can reduce uncertainty, surface context, or remove a repetitive step, it belongs in the flow. If it only makes a demo more impressive, it probably does not.
